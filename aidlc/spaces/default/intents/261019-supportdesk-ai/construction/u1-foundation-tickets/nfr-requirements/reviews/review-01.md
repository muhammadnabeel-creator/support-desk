### Summary

The NFR set is coherent, consistent with the functional rules and the contract summary, and appropriately small for a local single-user project. Two items would likely cause rework or a failed clean-clone check: the relative SQLite path (working-directory ambiguity) and enum strictness, where the stated "numbers rejected" and "missing is 400" behaviours are not what the framework does by default. The remaining items are minor tightening.
