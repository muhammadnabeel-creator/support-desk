### Summary

The design covers every NFR, and its layering is sound and implementable for a local single-user API. Two gaps would cause rework or runtime failures at code generation: the timestamp type on EF Core SQLite (ordering and the `Z` format) and the unspecified validation-error mapping. Neither is Critical, so the verdict is READY, but the human should weigh both Major findings before approving.
