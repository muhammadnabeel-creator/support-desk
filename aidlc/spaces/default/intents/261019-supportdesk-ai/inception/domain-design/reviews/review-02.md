### Summary

The McpServer component is sound: the graph is acyclic, ownership is single, and ADR-006 is complete. Two operating claims do not hold against the current code, the shared database path and the Groq key reaching a client-launched process. Fix those two in ADR-006 and remove the leftover Mock references.
