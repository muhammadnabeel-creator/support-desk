### Summary

The C5 tool list, result schemas, error-code lists, exclusions and C6 ownership are consistent with FR14, FR15 and ADR-006, with no hidden cycle. Two Major gaps remain that a developer would have to guess at: where argument validation and binding failures become isError results, and how the C1 response shapes and serializer options are shared. Both can be closed in the contract text without changing the design.
